## Project Origin (Maintainer Showcase — Not Counted as a Community Hire)

- **Creator:** Reda Salem ([@redasalem](https://github.com/redasalem)) · **Scope:** Global Remote / Full-Stack & AI Systems
- **Why this exists:** Built `veycraft` as a local-first, zero-dependency Markdown career operations framework around one principle: *AI can prepare your job applications, but it must never invent your experience or take control away from you.*
- **Reproducible technical evidence:** Run `npx github:redasalem/veycraft proof` or `npx github:redasalem/veycraft eval --baseline` locally to reproduce the deterministic fixture validation scorecard.
